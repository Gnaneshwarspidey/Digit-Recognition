9.3 Model Code (Keras)
from tensorflow.keras import layers, modelsdef build_cnn(num_classes):    model = models.Sequential([        layers.Input(shape=(28, 28, 1)),        layers.Conv2D(32, 3, padding='same'), layers.BatchNormalization(), layers.ReLU(),        layers.Conv2D(32, 3, padding='same'), layers.BatchNormalization(), layers.ReLU(),        layers.MaxPooling2D(), layers.Dropout(0.25),        layers.Conv2D(64, 3, padding='same'), layers.BatchNormalization(), layers.ReLU(),        layers.Conv2D(64, 3, padding='same'), layers.BatchNormalization(), layers.ReLU(),        layers.MaxPooling2D(), layers.Dropout(0.25),        layers.Flatten(),        layers.Dense(256, activation='relu'), layers.Dropout(0.5),        layers.Dense(num_classes, activation='softmax'),    ])    model.compile(optimizer='adam',                  loss='sparse_categorical_crossentropy',                  metrics=['accuracy'])    return model

=== 17. Viva Questions ===
17. Viva Questions & Answers
#
Question
Short answer
1
What is a CNN?
A neural network that uses convolution layers to automatically learn spatial features from images.
2
Why CNN instead of a plain MLP?
CNNs share weights, preserve spatial structure and need far fewer parameters, giving better accuracy on images.
3
What does a convolution layer do?
Slides filters over the image to detect local patterns like edges and curves.
4
Why pooling?
Reduces spatial size and computation, and gives small translation invariance.
5
Why ReLU?
Simple, reduces vanishing gradients, speeds up training.
6
Why softmax at the output?
Converts scores to probabilities across classes that sum to 1.
7
Which loss did you use and why?
Categorical cross-entropy, suited to multi-class classification with softmax.
8
What is dropout?
Randomly disables neurons during training to reduce overfitting.
9
What is batch normalisation?
Normalises layer inputs per batch, stabilising and speeding up training.
10
MNIST vs EMNIST?
MNIST has only digits (10 classes); EMNIST extends it with letters (and digits) in multiple splits.
11
Why are EMNIST images rotated?
They are stored in a transposed orientation, so they must be transposed back before use.
12
How did you prevent overfitting?
Dropout, batch norm, augmentation, early stopping, validation monitoring.
13
Why is accuracy lower on EMNIST?
More classes and many visually similar pairs (O/0, I/l, g/q).
14
What is a confusion matrix?
A table of actual vs predicted classes, revealing which classes are confused.
15
Why Adam optimiser?
Adaptive learning rates and momentum give fast, stable convergence.
16
Why is real hand-drawn accuracy lower than test accuracy?
Distribution shift in stroke width, position, background and noise; reduced by preprocessing and augmentation.
17
What is an epoch / batch size?
One full pass through the training data / number of samples per weight update.
18
How can you improve the model further?
Deeper networks, ensembles, more augmentation, transfer learning, tuning.
19
What are the limitations?
Single character only, 28×28 input, no cursive or multi-script support.
20
How will you extend it?
CRNN with CTC for words, regional scripts, TFLite for mobile.
